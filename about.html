Founded out of a passion for blending technical precision with vibrant personal style, Mikdraft Shop was created to transform traditional drafting supplies into aesthetically inspiring tools. Built on the idea that engineering, architecture, and design work don't have to be limited to plain or utilitarian gear, the shop curates high-quality drafting tools, measuring instruments, and drawing materials wrapped in signature pastel pink palettes. By combining professional functionality with a fresh, creative look, Mikdraft Shop empowers students, creators, and professionals to bring both precision and personality to their drafting tables.
